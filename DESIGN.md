## Brand & Style

This design system blends ethereal frosted glassmorphism with high-precision operational SaaS interfaces. Tailored for forward-thinking organizations, executive suites, and human resources platforms, it projects calm authority, clarity, and lightness.

The visual style departs from standard clinical enterprise software by pairing an iridescent, soft-gradient luminous pastel backdrop with translucent frosted cards, delicate borders, and pill-shaped geometry. The aesthetic feels breathable, weightless, and refined, balancing ergonomic data density with editorial whitespace.

Key brand attributes:
- **Luminous & Weightless:** Subtle gradient atmospheres, multi-layer frosted blur panels, and pristine white surfaces.
- **Precision Focus:** Crisp data tables, clock-in meters, and state capsules that make complex telemetry legible at a glance.
- **Harmonious Tactility:** Soft pill buttons, circular tracking rings, and organic chartreuse accents paired with deep obsidian grounding tokens.

## Colors

The palette uses a high-contrast core (deep obsidian `#121417` against translucent and frosted whites) accented by an electric soft lime/chartreuse (`#D8F25C`) and an optional cobalt tint (`#4C6BFF`) for focus and active states. 

### Role Assignments
- **Primary (`#121417`):** High-priority callouts, primary action buttons, active navigation pills, and dominant headline typography.
- **Secondary (`#D8F25C`):** High-energy indicator pills, progress milestones, radial timer highlights, and accent counters.
- **Tertiary (`#4C6BFF`):** Secondary interactive indicators, active timeline markers, and informational badges.
- **Neutrals (`#6C727F`, `#9EA3AE`, `#F0F2F5`):** Subdued metadata, column headers, inactive tab copy, and hairline borders.

### Surface System
- **Canvas Base:** Soft luminous multi-stop pastel wash: linear gradient transitioning from soft champagne mint (`#F3F7EE`) through pearl blue (`#EFF4FC`) to pale blush-lavender (`#F8F3FA`).
- **Surface Frosted Primary:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)` with a 24px backdrop blur and `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.65)` border overlay.
- **Surface Elevated Floating:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)` for dropdowns, popovers, and focused inspector panes.
- **Surface Subdued / Pill Inset:** `rgba(240, 243, 246, 0.60)` for inactive segmented pills and search enclosures.

### Feedback System
- **Success / On-Time:** Mint Green (`#16A34A` text on `#DCFCE7` pill container).
- **Warning / Pending / Invited:** Soft Solar Yellow (`#B45309` text on `#FEF3C7` pill container).
- **Critical / Absent:** Coral Red (`#DC2626` text on `#FEE2E2` pill container).

## Typography

This design system uses **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all typography levels. Its clean geometric construction, balanced curves, and refined counters pair with frosted glass cards and high-density telemetry tables.

### Rules of Rhythm
- **Stat Numerics:** Primary metrics (e.g., headcount, hours, percentages) use semi-bold and bold weights paired with small trailing percentage badges (`label-sm`).
- **Hierarchy:** Greeting headlines use `headline-lg` with loose letter-spacing (`-0.02em`).
- **Table Data:** Column headers use `caption` or `label-sm` with neutral secondary color and uppercase styling (`letter-spacing: 0.04em`). Numeric timestamps and records use tabular numerals for alignment.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout uses a modular, fluid 12-column grid system resting inside a padded frosted viewport frame.

### Layout Mechanics
- **Canvas Enclosure:** On large displays, the entire console is contained inside a central frosted container floating above the ambient pastel background, bordered with ample breathing margins (`margin-desktop: 2.5rem`).
- **Split-Pane Architecture:** Detail sections (e.g., profile inspection and attendance schedules) deploy an asymmetric split layout: a compact sticky sidecard (30% width / 3-4 columns) paired with an expansive data table area (70% width / 8-9 columns).
- **Card Clustering:** Dashboard telemetry cards use uniform `1.25rem` gaps with internal paddings of `space-lg` (`1.5rem`).
- **Responsive Adaptations:** On tablet and mobile viewports, the multi-column metrics and sidecard profile collapse into stacked full-width frosted modules with horizontal overflow scroll for tabs and tabular chronologies.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is achieved through translucency, frosted backdrop blurs, and luminous highlights rather than heavy drop shadows.

### Atmospheric Glassmorphism
- **Base Canvas:** Ambient pastel aura with subtle radial light blooms.
- **Level 1 (Card Containers):** Semi-transparent milky surface (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.70)`), background blur of `20px` to `28px`, hairline border of `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.80)`, and a soft ambient shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(18, 20, 23, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Pills, Quick Actions, Metric Badges):** Solid pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or obsidian (`#121417`), casting a subtle directional shadow: `0 4px 12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Popovers, Flyout Profile Drawers):** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)` surface blur (`32px`), framed by `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 1.0)`, with depth shadow `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(18, 20, 23, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The design system embraces an ultra-smooth rounded and capsule-driven shape language (`roundedness: 3`).

- **Containers & Glass Panels:** Rounded corners with a radius of `1.5rem` to `2rem` (24px - 32px), creating a friendly, organic frame.
- **Pill System:** All tabs, filter chips, action buttons, table status labels, and search bars use fully rounded pill profiles (`border-radius: 9999px`).
- **Avatars & Radial Trackers:** Circular form factors (`border-radius: 50%`) with high-precision internal dotted tracks or concentric strokes.
- **Table Rows & List Items:** Soft individual row groupings with `0.75rem` (12px) corner radii on hover states.

## Components

### 1. Navigation & Segmented Tabs
- **Console Header Navigation:** Floating white or frosted pill bar hosting pill triggers. Active tab renders in solid obsidian (`#121417`) with crisp white text; inactive tabs render in transparent glass with neutral text (`#6C727F`) and transition on hover to `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`.
- **Sub-View Tabs:** Pill group with smooth horizontal alignment (e.g., Attendance, Leave Quota, Employee Info, Settings) using pill shapes.

### 2. Buttons & Icon Actions
- **Primary Action:** Solid obsidian pill (`#121417`), white typography, padding `10px 20px`.
- **Secondary / Add Widget:** Frosted white pill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`), hairline white border, dark typography, with a leading plus icon (`+`).
- **Icon Quick-Triggers:** Circular buttons (`36px x 36px` to `40px x 40px`) in translucent glass, housing minimal monochromatic iconography (e.g., diagonal arrow indicator, phone, chat, download).

### 3. Status Badges & Chips
- **Micro Pills:** Height `22px` - `26px`, padding `2px 10px`, font size `11px`, bold weight.
- **Approved / Active / Onsite:** Lime-chartreuse tinted pill (`#D8F25C`) with dark label or emerald green tint (`#DCFCE7` / `#16A34A`).
- **Invited / Pending:** Solar yellow tint (`#FEF3C7` / `#B45309`).
- **Absent / Critical:** Delicate rose tint (`#FEE2E2` / `#DC2626`).

### 4. Cards & Profile Containers
- **Telemetry Metric Cards:** Frosted glass backing, large bold stat display, upward metric diff indicator pill, and top-right diagonal launch icon button.
- **Profile Inspector Card:** Centered rounded portrait avatar with active presence dot, bold profile title, role tag, direct-contact pill links (call, email), and an inset key-value detail list.

### 5. Tables & Record Lists
- **Structure:** Clean rows floating on frosted glass. Alternate rows may take a translucent wash (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.40)`).
- **Row Composition:** Employee checkbox, avatar thumbnail, name + role subtitle, monetary or hour data, date column, and an end-aligned status badge.
- **Attendance Timeline Rows:** Date marker, dual-column Clock In / Clock Out timestamps accompanied by directional diagonal arrows, and total working hour calculations.

### 6. Radial Timer & Circular Visualizers
- **Time Tracking Capsule:** Centered circular progress tracker featuring fine concentric dotted tick marks, an elapsed active arc, centered large digital countdown (`05.45`), pause and stop pill buttons, and subtle clock metadata icons.
